What a LocoBench number means
Each measurement is a tier floor, not a ranking.
If your card has at least this VRAM and at least this bandwidth, in the same or newer architecture, you will match or beat the result below.   same specs → same number.   newer architecture at same specs → better.   more of any spec → better.
